================================================================================
Inline markup in a paragraph
================================================================================

.   This is **bold** and *italic* and _underlined_

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(document
  (paragraph
    (inline_text
      (text)
      (bold (text_content))
      (text)
      (italic (text_content))
      (text)
      (underline (text_content)))))
