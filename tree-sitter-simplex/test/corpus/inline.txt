================================================================================
Inline markup in a paragraph
================================================================================

.   This is **bold** and *italic* and _underlined_

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(document
  (paragraph
    (inline_text
      (inline (text))
      (inline (bold (text_content)))
      (inline (text))
      (inline (italic (text_content)))
      (inline (text))
      (inline (underline (text_content))))))

================================================================================
Math, smallcaps and symbol

      (inline (symbol)))))
